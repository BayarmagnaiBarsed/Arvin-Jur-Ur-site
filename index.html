The assessment

An operational and maintenance gap assessment, built from a site walk

We look at three areas. Each finding is tied to an action, the tool to do it with, and a priority, so the result is a work plan and not a report that sits on a shelf.

Safety and compliance

Remove personnel hazards, structural liabilities and regulatory risk.

  • Missing guards and edge protection
  • Structural integrity and inspection plans
  • Substandard work platforms
  • General workplace hazards

Mechanical reliability

Stop repeat failure loops and raise precision maintenance skills.

  • Freezing lines and gearboxes
  • V-belt failures
  • Cracked shafts and structural metals
  • Fan breakdowns and balancing
  • High or unexplained vibration

Operations and asset management

Modernise material flow, software tracking and communication.

  • Preventive maintenance planning
  • Liner and pipe wear rates
  • Breakdown registration for all equipment
  • Chutes, seals, lagging and belt tracking
  • Shutdown activity lists and team communication

  1. Site walk-down

    Inspect conveyors, fixed equipment, guarding, platforms, structures and stores on site.

  2. Findings and recommendations

    Every observation is paired with a recommended action, an engineering solution where one is needed, and the tool to carry it out.

  3. Rank the work

    Ranked by safety risk and downtime impact.Must-doShould-doCould-do

    Quick wins in 0–3 months, medium term in 3–12 months, longer-term studies after 12 months.

Condition monitoring assessment

Five checks per critical asset:

  1. Can a known technique detect the dominant failure mode?
  2. Is it installed or scheduled on this asset?
  3. Is it sampled often enough to catch the warning period?
  4. Is the data trended in one place?
  5. Does an alarm lead to planned work?

Each asset scores level 0 to 3, from no monitoring to alarms that produce planned work with parts ready.

How condition monitoring moves reliability

Reliability = MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR). Illustrative example for one critical asset: an MTBF of 157.7 hours and an MTTR of 6.3 hours give 96.1%.

CaseMTBF (h)MTTR (h)Reliability
Today157.76.396.1%
MTTR cut to 5 h only157.75.096.9%
MTBF raised to 300 h only3006.397.9%
MTBF 300 h and MTTR 5 h3005.098.4%

Raising MTBF does more than cutting MTTR, so early warning of failures is the bigger lever. Incident downtime only; planned shutdowns are covered separately.
